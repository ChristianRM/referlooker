Sr.Automation QA with expertise in Functionize (IRC300775)
Mexico, Mexico City
Job Description

Design and implement comprehensive test automation strategies for complex software systems within an enterprise environment.
Develop and maintain robust automation frameworks using industry-standard tools, with a specific focus on Functionize capabilities.
Lead the creation of detailed test plans, test cases, and test scripts for various application components and features.
Contribute to the architectural design of scalable and maintainable automation solutions that integrate seamlessly with existing infrastructure.
Mentor junior QA engineers on best practices in test automation, quality assurance methodologies, and tool utilization.
Perform in-depth analysis of test results to identify defects, performance bottlenecks, and areas for continuous improvement.
Collaborate effectively with development, product, and other cross-functional teams to ensure high-quality software delivery throughout the SDLC.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives for test processes, automation coverage, and overall quality assurance effectiveness.
Evaluate and recommend new testing technologies, tools, and approaches to enhance efficiency and effectiveness of the QA function.
Ensure strict adherence to quality standards, regulatory requirements, and internal guidelines in all testing activities.
Troubleshoot and resolve complex issues within the automation framework, test environments, and integrated systems.
Provide technical guidance and support for integrating automated tests into continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines.
